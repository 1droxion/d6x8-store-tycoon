# Tycoon: City Life — Support

Run a neighborhood shop, stock products, serve customers, hire staff, and explore the city.

## Playing on iPhone and iPad

- Drag the circular joystick at the bottom left to move. Drag the right side of the game to look around.
- Walk close to a usable object, then tap its action prompt.
- Tap the bottom three-dot menu for your phone, taxi, map, camera, and control settings.
- Choose Eye view or Full body view from the menu to change the camera.
- Your progress saves on the device. Keep the app installed to preserve your local save.
