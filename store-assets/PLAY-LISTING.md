# Store Tycoon: City Life

## Short description
Build your store, hire staff, stock shelves, and explore a lively 3D city.

## Full description
Your little shop. Your next big move.

Start with a neighborhood grocery store and build your own daily routine in Store Tycoon: City Life, a colorful 3D store and city simulation from D6X8.

RUN YOUR STORE
Open the doors, serve customers, scan their baskets, accept payments, and return the right change. Set product prices and keep popular groceries in stock.

HANDLE DELIVERIES
Order supplies from your store computer. Meet the delivery truck, carry boxes inside, and stock the matching shelves.

BUILD YOUR TEAM
Hire cashiers, stockers, and other workers. Watch your stocker collect delivery boxes and carry them to the shelves. Manage wages as your business grows.

GROW YOUR BUSINESS
Reinvest fictional game earnings in store improvements and properties. Progress at your own pace and keep building your neighborhood business.

EXPLORE CITY LIFE
Walk, run, travel by taxi, and drive available vehicles. Visit your home, rest, watch the in-game TV, eat at restaurants, and meet simulated city residents.

MAKE THE CONTROLS YOURS
Adjust the controls and graphics to suit your device. Your store progress saves on this device.

OPTIONAL REWARDS
Adults who choose to enable ads can watch optional rewarded ads for fictional store cash. Core gameplay does not require watching ads. Ad availability varies.

IMPORTANT
This is a single-player simulation. All money, banking, stocks, businesses and rewards are fictional game systems with no real-money value or cash-out. This release does not include online multiplayer or real-money purchases. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove progress. A compatible device with WebGL support is required; performance varies by device.
