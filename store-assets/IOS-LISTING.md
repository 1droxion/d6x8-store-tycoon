## Description
Start with a neighborhood shop and grow it one decision at a time.

Carry deliveries inside, stock matching shelves, set product prices, and serve customers at the checkout. Scan purchases and give the right cash change. Hire help as your business grows and expand your store.

Take a break from the counter and explore the neighborhood. Visit your home, try a meal, call a taxi, drive vehicles, or use the city overview to find your way around.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Stock products and manage your store.
• Scan purchases and handle checkout payments.
• Hire staff to help with daily work.
• Explore streets, visit buildings, and travel around the city.
• Switch between full-body and eye views.
• Use a circular movement joystick and nearby action prompts.
• Adjust controls and graphics for your device.

This iPhone and iPad release is single-player. Progress saves on your device. All balances, purchases, properties, and rewards are fictional game currency with no cash value.
