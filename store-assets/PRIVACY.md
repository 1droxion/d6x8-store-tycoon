# Privacy Policy — Store Tycoon: City Life

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Store Tycoon: City Life (`com.d6x8.storetycoon`) is published by D6X8 / Dhruv Patel. This policy describes the Android game. The separate browser demo has its own hosted sign-in and online-presence behavior and is not the Android game.

## Gameplay data on your device

The Android game stores your shop name, fictional store balance, inventory, upgrades, game progress, control preferences, and optional-ad age-group choice on your device. The Android release does not offer account registration, chat, online multiplayer, or cloud game saves. These gameplay records are not sent to our own server. Your device's backup settings may include app data in Android system backups. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove progress.

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or photo-library access. All store balances, bank and stock simulations, property prices and rewards are fictional game currency. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, exchanged or transferred to other players.

## Optional advertising

Adults who choose to enable optional rewarded ads can watch an ad for the clearly stated in-game reward. Players can continue core gameplay without watching ads. Players who select Under 18 or Prefer not to say are not offered ads. That age-group choice stays on the device; we do not ask for a birth date.

Advertising uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. When you enable optional ads, Google may collect and share data such as IP address and approximate location derived from it, advertising/device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver, measure and secure ads and prevent fraud. Collection and personalization depend on your region, device settings and consent choices. We do not sell your gameplay records. Google processes ad data under its own policies and may act as an independent controller.

Before requesting ads, the app requests current consent information and shows a consent form where required. You can revisit available consent options through Phone → Online & store → Ad privacy choices (called Rewards & privacy in newer menus). You can stop using optional ads from that screen. Your device may also provide advertising-ID and ad privacy controls. A temporary ad or network error does not stop core gameplay.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google partner-site/app data use: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Children

The game is intended for players aged 16 and older, and is not directed to children. Optional ads are offered only after an 18-or-older confirmation. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our own servers.

## Storage, security and deletion

Gameplay records remain in the app's local storage until you reset the game, clear app data, or uninstall. Game reset may leave control preferences or ad-consent settings; use Android's Clear storage control to remove all app-local data. Network requests to advertising services use their SDKs' encrypted transport. No online system or device storage is guaranteed completely secure.

We do not maintain a user account database for the Android game. To exercise rights concerning Google's advertising data, use the Google privacy controls and contact details in Google's privacy policy. Retention of ad-related data follows Google's policies. The developer cannot directly delete records independently held by Google.

## Contact and changes

For developer support or a privacy request, use https://github.com/1droxion/d6x8-store-tycoon/issues and mention Store Tycoon: City Life. Do not post private or sensitive personal information in a public issue. You can also use the developer contact displayed on the game's Google Play listing once published.

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
