# D6X8 Store Tycoon — launch plan

Public browser beta: https://d6x8-store-tycoon.dhruv7935.chatgpt.site

## Current release
3D city/store simulation, checkout and exact change, deliveries, hired staff and stocker animations, homes and restaurants, NPCs, vehicles, taxi/map, progression, responsive/customizable controls and adaptive graphics.

## Next checks
1. Test Safari on real iPhones, Chrome on Android, tablet/foldable rotation and compatible TV browsers/controllers.
2. Measure frame rate, memory, loading time and long sessions; fix issues reported by beta players.
3. Confirm save recovery and online presence under realistic use. Economy progress currently stays on the device; online presence requires sign-in.
4. Build and test Android/iOS packages before app-store submission.
5. Choose monetization and connect real ads/payments only after platform requirements and testing.

Real ads and payments are not connected, and revenue is not guaranteed. This release is a browser beta; universal device compatibility and zero glitches are not certified.

Detailed development checklist: public/game/game-plan.html. Device implementation/testing notes: DEVICE-BETA.md.
